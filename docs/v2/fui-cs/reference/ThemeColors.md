# ThemeColors

record in `FuiCs`

The theme's palette.

## Properties

- `Accent`: The accent: primary buttons, checked controls, focus and selection highlights.
- `AccentHovered`: The accent while hovered.
- `AccentPressed`: The accent while pressed.
- `Background`: The page behind everything.
- `Border`: Field and panel borders.
- `DialogBackdrop`: The veil over the page behind a modal dialog.
- `DialogShadow`: A dialog card's shadow.
- `FocusRing`: The keyboard focus ring.
- `PanelShadow`: A floating panel's shadow.
- `ScrollbarThumb`: The scroll bar's thumb.
- `ScrollbarTrack`: The scroll bar's track.
- `Selection`: The text selection highlight.
- `Surface`: Cards, panels and fields.
- `TextMuted`: Secondary and disabled text.
- `TextOnAccent`: Text on an accent fill.
- `TextPrimary`: Body text.

## Methods

- `Equals(ThemeColors)`: Compares by reference, as every theme record does (see [`Theme`](Theme.md)).
- `GetHashCode()`: The reference's hash, matching the reference equality.
